1. Import Starter Questionnaire

One click uploads the default 60-item Fundamentals of Accounting set (20 multiple choice, 10 true/false, 10 identification, 20 problem-solving), plus the standard four sections, to your question bank. Safe to re-run — it overwrites your whole question bank and section list with the defaults.

2. Manage Sections (Parts)

Add a brand-new section, reorder sections, rename them, or toggle any section on/off — turning a section off hides it from students immediately without deleting its questions. Click Save Sections when done.

3. Upload Your Own Questionnaire

Paste your own questions below using this format (any number of items). This replaces your entire question bank and section list with the standard four sections.

For True or False, just write each statement as its own paragraph — students will see True/False choices automatically, no a)/b) needed.

4. Edit Questions

Edit text and choices, add new items with "+ Add Item," delete a single item with its "Delete" button, or clear a whole section's items with "Delete Entire Section's Items." Nothing changes for students until you click Save All Changes.
