1. Import Starter Questionnaire

One click uploads the default 60-item Fundamentals of Accounting set (20 multiple choice, 10 true/false, 10 identification, 20 problem-solving) to the question bank. Safe to re-run — it overwrites your whole question bank with the defaults.

2. Upload Your Own Questionnaire

Paste your own questions below using this format (any number of items — doesn't have to be 50). This replaces your entire question bank.

For True or False, just write each statement as its own paragraph — students will see True/False choices automatically, no a)/b) needed.

3. Edit Questions

Edit text and choices, add new items with "+ Add Item," delete a single item with its "Delete" button, or clear a whole section with "Delete Entire Section." Nothing changes for students until you click Save All Changes.
